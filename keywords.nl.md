# Keyword research and page map — Dutch (nl) — volumeboosting.com/nl/

Goal: same as `keywords.md` — rank for Dutch queries (Netherlands and Flanders) from people with a
quiet file on their iPhone right now. Copy uses informal «je» per `glossary.tsv`.

Method (2026-10-02): the NL App Store listing (localized: "Audioversterker – Volume Boost" /
"Geluid harder maken"), the phrases the listing targets (audioversterker, volume versterken, geluid
harder maken), Dutch query patterns («… harder maken», «… te zacht»), Apple's Dutch app names where
verifiable (support.apple.com/nl-nl), and the English map. No keyword-volume tool; tiers are
directional (Dutch ≈ 1/15–1/25 of US English demand; NL iPhone share is high). Validate in Search
Console (filter `/nl/`).

Tiers: **High** ≈ 500+/mo, **Medium** ≈ 100–500, **Low** ≈ 20–100, **Niche** < 20.
