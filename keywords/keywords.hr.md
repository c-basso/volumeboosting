# Keyword research and page map — Croatian (hr) — volumeboosting.com/hr/

Goal: same as `keywords.md` — rank for Croatian queries (Croatia, plus Bosnian/Serbian Latin
searchers who share most terms) from people with a quiet file on their iPhone right now.
Copy uses informal «ti» per `glossary.tsv`.

Method (2026-10-02): Croatian SERP checks, the HR App Store listing (`?l=hr`, localized:
"Pojačaj Glasnoću Zvuka" / "Za video, glazbu i snimke"), Apple's Croatian app names where verifiable
(support.apple.com/hr-hr), and the English map. No keyword-volume tool; tiers are directional
(Croatian ≈ 1/40–1/60 of US English demand). Validate in Search Console (filter `/hr/`).

Tiers: **High** ≈ 300+/mo, **Medium** ≈ 50–300, **Low** ≈ 10–50, **Niche** < 10.

## 0. What the HR storefront shows (2026-10-02)

- Croatian title/subtitle and description; not enough ratings to show a score.
- **Screenshots are not localized**: the Croatian listing serves the same 6 English images as other
  non-localized stores. The site keeps the shared `/img/screenshots/1–7.webp`; no `img/screenshots/hr/`.
- IAPs (HR): weekly subscription with trial; annual subscription 29,90 €. No lifetime option, so
  Croatian copy says «pretplata» only.
- The store title is an imperative («Pojačaj Glasnoću Zvuka»); copy uses it as a proper name
  («aplikacija Pojačaj Glasnoću Zvuka»). Older site brand line "Increase Volume – Pojačanje zvuka" remains
  in hero/structured data.

## 1. SERP observations

- "kako pojačati zvuk videa na iPhoneu": no Croatian answer page; results are English content farms
  → strong opportunity with modest absolute volume.
- Phrasing: «pojačati zvuk / glasnoću», «pretih», «tih», «ne čuje se», «glasnije».
- Apple Croatian names used: Fotografije, Datoteke, **Diktafon** (verified on support.apple.com/hr-hr;
  `glossary.tsv` says «glasovne bilješke» — update the glossary), Kontrolni centar, Snimanje zaslona.
- **Verify on a Croatian iPhone** (not confirmed from Apple docs): «Postavke › Zvukovi i dodir ›
  Sigurnost slušalica › Smanji glasan zvuk», «Postavke › Glazba › Provjera zvuka», EQ «Kasna noć»,
  «Pristupačnost › Zvuk i vizualno › Balans», «Face ID i šifra», «Vrijeme uporabe», «Poboljšaj snimku».
