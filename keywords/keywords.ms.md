# Keyword research and page map — Malay (ms) — volumeboosting.com/ms/

Goal: same as `keywords.md` — rank for Malay queries (Malaysia, Brunei, Singapore Malay speakers)
from people with a quiet file on their iPhone right now. Copy uses polite «anda» per `glossary.tsv`.
Malaysian searchers mix English loanwords (voice note, screen record, volume).

Method (2026-10-02): the MY App Store listing (`?l=ms`, localized: "Penguat Volume Bunyi" /
"Muzik & Video Lebih Kuat"), the phrases the listing targets (penguat volume, penguat bunyi, penguat
audio, penguat volume video), Malay query patterns («cara kuatkan …», «… perlahan»), Apple's Malay app
names where verifiable (support.apple.com/ms-my), and the English map. No keyword-volume tool; tiers
are directional (Malay ≈ 1/30–1/50 of US English demand). Validate in Search Console (filter `/ms/`).

Tiers: **High** ≈ 300+/mo, **Medium** ≈ 50–300, **Low** ≈ 10–50, **Niche** < 10.

## 0. What the MY storefront shows (2026-10-02)

- Malay title/subtitle and description; not enough ratings to display.
- The listing says «Photos» in English (iOS Malay name is «Foto»), and the Terms/Privacy labels are in
  English — small fixes for App Store Connect.
- **Screenshots are not localized** (same 6 English images as he/hr/hu/id/ja/ko). The site keeps the
  shared `/img/screenshots/1–7.webp`; no `img/screenshots/ms/`.
- IAPs (MY): weekly subscription with free trial; annual subscription RM50.90. Subscription only.
