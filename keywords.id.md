# Keyword research and page map — Indonesian (id) — volumeboosting.com/id/

Goal: same as `keywords.md` — rank for Indonesian queries from people with a quiet file on their
iPhone right now. Indonesia is a large, mobile-first market; volumes are meaningfully higher than
the other small locales. Copy uses polite «Anda» per `glossary.tsv`.

Method (2026-10-02): the ID App Store listing (`?l=id`, localized: "Penguat Volume – Volume Boost" /
"Musik dan video lebih keras"), common Indonesian search phrasing («cara mengeraskan …», «VN WA
pelan»), Apple's Indonesian app names where verifiable (support.apple.com/id-id), and the English
map. No keyword-volume tool; tiers are directional (Indonesian ≈ 1/5–1/10 of US English demand for
these intents). Validate in Search Console (filter `/id/`).

Tiers: **High** ≈ 1k+/mo, **Medium** ≈ 200–1k, **Low** ≈ 50–200, **Niche** < 50.

## 1. Language notes

- Phrasing: «cara mengeraskan suara …», «memperbesar volume», «suara pelan / kecil», «VN / voice
  note WA», «rekam layar / screen record». Users mix English loanwords (voice note, screen record).
- Apple Indonesian names used: Foto, File, **Memo Suara** (verified on support.apple.com/id-id),
  Pusat Kontrol, Perekaman Layar, iMovie.
- **Verify on an Indonesian iPhone** (not confirmed from Apple docs): «Pengaturan › Suara & Haptik ›
  Keamanan Headphone › Kurangi Audio Keras», «Musik › Periksa Suara», EQ «Larut Malam»,
  «Aksesibilitas › Audio & Visual › Keseimbangan», «Face ID & Kode Sandi», «Durasi Layar»,
  «Tingkatkan Rekaman».
