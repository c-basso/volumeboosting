# Keyword research and page map — Italian (it) — volumeboosting.com/it/

Goal: same as `keywords.md` — rank for Italian queries from people with a quiet file on their
iPhone right now. Copy uses informal «tu» per `glossary.tsv`.

Method (2026-10-02): the IT App Store listing (localized: "Aumenta Volume" / "Amplificatore Musica
e Video"), the phrases the listing itself targets («aumenta volume», «aumentare il volume»,
«amplificatore di volume», «potenziatore di volume»), Apple's Italian app names where verifiable
(support.apple.com/it-it), and the English map. No keyword-volume tool; tiers are directional
(Italian ≈ 1/8–1/12 of US English demand). Validate in Search Console (filter `/it/`).

Tiers: **High** ≈ 1k+/mo, **Medium** ≈ 200–1k, **Low** ≈ 50–200, **Niche** < 50.
