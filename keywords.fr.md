# Keyword research and page map — French (fr) — volumeboosting.com/fr/

Goal: same as `keywords.md` — rank for French queries (France, Belgium, Switzerland, Québec,
francophone Africa) from people with a quiet file on their iPhone right now.

Method (2026-10-02): French SERP checks per query, the FR App Store listing (localized:
"Amplificateur de Volume Audio" / "Volume Boost – Son plus fort"), Apple's French app and
settings names, and the English map. No keyword-volume tool; tiers are directional (French
combined ≈ 1/5–1/7 of US English demand). Validate in Search Console (filter `/fr/`).

Tiers: **High** ≈ 2k+/mo, **Medium** ≈ 300–2k, **Low** ≈ 50–300, **Niche** < 50.

## 0. What the FR storefront shows (2026-10-02)

- Localized title/subtitle; 4.5★ from 8 ratings. French screenshots now in `img/screenshots/fr/`.

## 1. SERP observations

- "augmenter le son d'une vidéo iPhone": Filmora/Wondershare, Clipchamp, Apeaksoft, Coolmuster
  (vendor pages pushing desktop tools) + generic articles. No page answers the iPhone file-boost
  intent natively.
- "enregistrement d'écran son trop faible": Apple Communauté threads («Son très bas durant
  l'enregistrement d'écran iPhone») with no real fix → strong opportunity.
- "message vocal WhatsApp trop faible": Android-centric results and news; iPhone page has room.
- Phrasing: «augmenter le son / le volume», «rendre plus fort», «son trop faible / trop bas»,
  «amplifier». Copy uses «vous» (per `glossary.tsv`).
- Apple's French names used: Photos, Fichiers, Dictaphone (Voice Memos), Messages, Centre de
  contrôle, Enregistrement de l'écran, Réglages › Sons et vibrations › Sécurité des écouteurs ›
  Réduire les sons forts, Réglages › Musique › Égaliseur de volume (Sound Check), Améliorer
  l'enregistrement. **Verify** the EQ preset name on a French iPhone.
