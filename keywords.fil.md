# Keyword research and page map — Filipino (fil) — volumeboosting.com/fil/

Goal: same as `keywords.md` — rank for Filipino/Taglish queries from people with a quiet file on
their iPhone right now, and send them to the App Store.

Method (2026-10-02): SERP checks for Filipino/Taglish queries, the PH App Store storefront
(`country=ph`), and the English map. No keyword-volume tool; tiers are directional. Philippine
users search in **Taglish** (Tagalog grammar + English tech words) and in plain English; the English
guides already cover the English queries and are hreflang-linked to these pages.

Tiers: **Medium** ≈ 200+/mo, **Low** ≈ 30–200, **Niche** < 30.

## 0. What the PH storefront shows (2026-10-02)

- English listing (App Store has no Filipino metadata locale). 4.75★ from 4 ratings, PHP pricing.
- Screenshots/icon identical to US. Nothing to download.
- iOS has no Filipino system language → Apple UI names stay English in the copy (Photos, Files,
  Voice Memos, Control Center, Settings › Sounds & Haptics › Headphone Safety › Reduce Loud Audio).

## 1. SERP observations

- Taglish queries («paano lakasan ang volume ng video sa iPhone», «mahina ang tunog ng screen
  record») return **only English pages** (Apple Community, Adobe, Clipchamp help) — there is no
  Filipino-language answer at all. A Taglish page is the only native result.
- Phrasing: «lakasan» / «palakasin» (make louder), «mahina ang tunog / volume» (sound is weak),
  «paano» (how to). Tech nouns stay English: video, voice memo, screen record, MP3, volume.
- **Messenger** is the dominant chat app in the Philippines (followed by Viber, WhatsApp,
  Telegram); the voice-message guide leads with Messenger and explains its export limitation.
- Copy uses informal «mo/ka» in Taglish, matching `build/fil.json`.
