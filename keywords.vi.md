# Keyword research — Vietnamese (vi)

Market: Vietnam. Searches are Vietnamese with diacritics (often typed without), plus Latin platform names (iPhone, Zalo, TikTok, MP3). Core phrases: tăng âm lượng, làm to hơn, khuếch đại âm thanh; problem words: tiếng nhỏ, nhỏ, mất tiếng, bị rè.

## Store facts (apps.apple.com/vn/app/id6741472421?l=vi, checked 2026-10-04)
- Listing IS localized: title **Tăng Âm Lượng Video**, subtitle «Khuếch Đại Nhạc & Thu Âm», category Nhạc, language field VI (correct).
- VN storefront rating 5,0★ (4 ratings); subscription price shown 209.000đ (annual) + weekly plan. Site still shows global 4.5★/121 and a lifetime price_note — verify.
- Age rating renders as "00+" on the VN page (Apple display glitch?) — check App Store Connect.
- Screenshots: shared English set (7bbadc97…).
- vi.json body uses brand "Increase Volume – Tăng cường âm thanh"; guides/FAQ/meta use the store brand «Tăng Âm Lượng Video».
- Apple VN names verified (support.apple.com/vi-vn): **Ghi âm** (Voice Memos), Cài đặt, Tệp, Ảnh, Chia sẻ. Glossary agrees.
- Glossary locale_voice prefers avoiding personal pronouns; guides mostly avoid them but use «bạn» in a few places.
