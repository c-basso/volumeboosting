# Keyword research and page map — German (de) — volumeboosting.com/de/

Goal: same as `keywords.md` — rank for German queries from people with a quiet file on their
iPhone right now, and send them to the App Store. Germany + Austria + Switzerland is the largest
non-English market for this site, so German gets real search demand.

Method (2026-10-01): German SERP checks for each candidate query, the DE App Store listing
(which is localized: "Audioverstärker – Volume Boost / Videos und Musik lauter machen"), Apple's
German app and settings names, and the English map. No keyword-volume tool was used; tiers are
directional (DACH ≈ 1/6–1/8 of US English demand for these intents). Validate in Search
Console (filter: page contains `/de/`) after 4–6 weeks.

Tiers: **High** ≈ 2k+/mo, **Medium** ≈ 300–2k, **Low** ≈ 50–300, **Niche** < 50.

## 0. What the DE storefront shows (2026-10-01)

- Localized title/subtitle: **Audioverstärker – Volume Boost** / **Videos und Musik lauter machen**.
  The website's German brand line ("Increase Volume – Klangboost") differs from the store title;
  the German pages mention "Audioverstärker" in titles so searchers recognise the store listing.
- DE rating: 2.3★ from 3 ratings. Same expectation-mismatch risk as elsewhere; every page states
  that the app makes the file louder, not the speaker.
- **Localized screenshots** (German headlines) — now in `img/screenshots/de/`. Screenshot 2 says
  "Video **louder** machen" (mixed English/German): fix in App Store Connect.

## 1. SERP observations

- "Video lauter machen iPhone", "Bildschirmaufnahme Ton zu leise", "MP3 lauter machen iPhone" are
  dominated by vendor blogs (Filmora/Wondershare, EaseUS, Apeaksoft, VideoProc), Tech-Tipp
  portals (GIGA, heise, Macwelt, schieb.de) and Apple Community threads. Vendor pages push desktop
  software; Tech-Tipp pages explain settings but not file boosting. A specific iPhone-file answer
  in native German has room to rank.
- "WhatsApp Sprachnachricht zu leise" has strong Tech-Tipp coverage (heise, GIGA, antwort.net)
  focused on the earpiece/proximity-sensor issue; our guide covers that *and* the file fix.
- German phrasing: "lauter machen" (most common action phrase), "Lautstärke erhöhen", "verstärken",
  "zu leise" (problem phrase). Copy uses all three; slugs use "lauter-machen" / "zu-leise".
- Apple's German names used: Fotos, Dateien, Sprachmemos, Nachrichten, Kontrollzentrum,
  Bildschirmaufnahme, Einstellungen › Töne & Haptik › Kopfhörersicherheit › Laute Töne reduzieren,
  Einstellungen › Musik › Lautstärke anpassen (Sound Check), Aufnahme verbessern.

### Deliberately excluded

- "iPhone Lautsprecher lauter machen", "iPhone lauter machen" (speaker/system intent): the app
  cannot satisfy it. Every page says once that it boosts the file, not the speaker.
- "Lautstärke Verstärker Android", "Chrome Volume Booster": wrong platform.
- "Spotify / YouTube lauter": streamed, DRM-protected.
